## Archivo

FATHOM bundles [Archivo](https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo) by
Omnibus-Type, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Five static instances ship:
Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold at normal width for UI and numerals, and
SemiExpanded SemiBold for section titles and readout values. The complete
license text is shipped at `Fathom/Resources/Fonts/LICENSE-Archivo.txt` and
inside the application bundle.
