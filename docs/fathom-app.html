Your SSD is fine, and most of what you have read about Apple silicon SSD wear is wrong. This drive has written 149.97 TB and given up 5% of its endurance. Straight-line, that is 38,500 power-on hours. This Mac has been on for 1,925 of them across 520 cycles, so the honest answer is decades, not a date. We are not going to print “March 2030” and let you worry about it, because Apple does not publish a TBW rating for the AP0512Z and any specific date would be a guess dressed as a forecast. If the rate changes, the digest will tell you. Until then, this screen is allowed to be boring.
